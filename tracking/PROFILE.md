## Evidence-based status rules (coaching defaults)
- UNTESTED: no diagnostic evidence.
- LEARNING: concept introduced; independent application not yet reliable.
- REPAIR: observed difficulty; state error type and sample size.
- PRACTISING: independent answers improving; delayed/timed evidence pending.
- RETAINED: at least 10 unseen independent attempts across 2 sessions, >=80% correct, explain-back successful, and later recall/application check passed. Use verified exam timing where applicable.
These thresholds are coaching rules, not DSSSB qualifying criteria. Always show denominator and hint/guess flags. Reopen retained topics after repeated errors.

## What to capture
Independent accuracy; all-assigned completion; median time; explain-back; formula recall and selection; delayed retention.
Correct guess/hint-assisted responses are not mastery evidence.
Small samples remain tentative. A lapse should not become a permanent weak-subject label.
