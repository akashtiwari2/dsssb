# Provisional calendar

Target assumption: 1 December 2026. Official date/year, post and daily hours pending.
From 30 October through 30 November there are 32 full calendar days; 29 October is setup day. This is a preparation structure, not a claim that an unidentified syllabus is fully coverable.

| Dates | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 29–30 Oct | Exact exam verification, syllabus mapping, baseline | Verified scheme; topic checklist; initial priority and capacity |
| 31 Oct–10 Nov | Highest priority concepts and prerequisites with PYQs | Daily lessons, explained solutions, formula cards, error repair |
| 11–20 Nov | Remaining important coverage and mixed practice | Section tests, gap list, spaced recall |
| 21–26 Nov | Exam-length practice and targeted repair | Mocks in verified format; analysed errors; revised priorities |
| 27–29 Nov | Consolidation and final recall checks | Short revision sheets; recurring mistakes; attempt strategy |
| 30 Nov | Light recall and exam logistics | Formula/conditions recall, familiar errors, rest |

Daily hours milne par har din ke exact topics aur minutes assign honge. Agar intake late aaye to purane dates ki fake completion nahi; remaining days se plan rebuild karo.

## Priority method
1. Officially tested sections, marks, sectional requirements aur prerequisites identify karo.
2. Relevant papers ka topic-wise count / total analysed questions record karo; sample size aur post differences disclose karo.
3. Frequent/scoring topic + measured weakness = early attention. Essential prerequisite ko pehle repair karo.
4. Untested topic ko weak ya low priority mat label karo.
5. Har section ka minimum coverage reserve karo; narrow PYQ repetition se poora syllabus covered mat maano.
6. Har 3 study sessions ya meaningful test result ke baad plan reassess karo. Backlog aaye to transparent reschedule karo.

## Initial daily time split (coaching default, adjustable)
- 15% due revision and closed-book formula recall.
- 35% concepts and worked examples.
- 35% PYQs/unseen application.
- 15% error repair, short test and notes.
Mock days par full paper aur analysis ko adequate time do; fixed question quota capacity ke bina impose mat karo.

## Revision cycle
First independent learning ke baad next day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days; failures par earlier retry. Exam se pehle dates compress karo. Due dates actual attempt dates se calculate karo.
Har recall mein formula/meaning ke saath application bhi check karo.

## Practical suggestion
Ek topic ko bahut saari websites se dobara padhne ki jagah official scope + ek clear explanation + verified question practice + error log use karo. Mock dene ke baad galtiyon ka repair next session mein karo.
